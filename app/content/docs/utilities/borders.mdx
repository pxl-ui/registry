PXL-UI includes a `pixel-border` utility that can be used to display retro styled borders. This works together with the `pixel-color-*` and `pixel-size-*` utilities.


<div class="not-content space-y-2">
  <div class="flex flex-col sm:flex-row gap-2 items-center">
    <h2 class="font-sans text-xl sm:w-30">sm</h2>
    <p class="pixel-border pixel-color-border pixel-size-sm w-full h-10"></p>
  </div>
  <div class="flex flex-col sm:flex-row gap-2 items-center">
    <h2 class="font-sans text-xl sm:w-30">md</h2>
    <p class="pixel-border pixel-color-border pixel-size-md w-full h-10"></p>
  </div>
  <div class="flex flex-col sm:flex-row gap-2 items-center">
    <h2 class="font-sans text-xl sm:w-30">lg</h2>
    <p class="pixel-border pixel-color-border pixel-size-lg w-full h-10"></p>
  </div>
  <div class="flex flex-col sm:flex-row gap-2 items-center">
    <h2 class="font-sans text-xl sm:w-30">colored</h2>
    <p class="pixel-border pixel-color-red-500 pixel-size-md w-full h-10"></p>
  </div>
</div>
