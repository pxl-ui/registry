Soothing pastel theme for the high-spirited!

[Catppuccin](https://catppuccin.com/palette/) consists of 4 beautiful pastel color palettes, named flavors. The theme comes in one light and three dark variants:

* **Latte**: Lightest theme harmoniously inverting the essence of Catppuccin's dark themes.
* <a href={url("colors/catppuccin-frappe")}>Frappé</a>: Less vibrant alternative using subdued colors for a muted aesthetic.
* <a href={url("colors/catppuccin-macchiato")}>Macchiato</a>: Medium contrast with gentle colors creating a soothing atmosphere.
* <a href={url("colors/catppuccin-mocha")}>Mocha</a>: The Original — Darkest variant offering a cozy feeling with color-rich accents.
