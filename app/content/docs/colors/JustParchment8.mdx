A small 8 colour palette made for paper and ink / parchment style designs. To be used for things like old scrolls and maps and to give the feel of old fasioned ink on parchment drawings. 
Made it by taking various stock images of old scrolls and maps and condencing the colours down then tweaking them to work with my sprites. Created by [JustJimmy](https://lospec.com/just-jimmy)
